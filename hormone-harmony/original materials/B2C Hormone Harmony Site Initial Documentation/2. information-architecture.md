## Page 1: Homepage (/)

### Purpose
Convert visitors into engaged users by clearly communicating value, building trust, and guiding to primary CTA (assessment/waitlist).

### Target Audience
Women 25-55 experiencing hormone-related symptoms, seeking understanding and solutions.

### Success Metrics
- Time on page > 60 seconds
- Scroll depth > 75%
- Primary CTA click-through > 15%
- Bounce rate < 40%

**Layout:** Full-width, centered content, gradient background

**Content:**
- **Headline (H1):** "Find Your Hormone Balance"
- **Subheadline:** "Evidence-based guidance and support for every stage of your hormonal journey"
- **Byline:** "Powered by Rhithm technology" (small, elegant text below subheadline)
- **Primary CTA:** Button - "Start Your Journey" (links to assessment or waitlist)
- **Secondary CTA:** Button (outline) - "Learn More" (smooth scroll to next section)
- **Hero Image:** Warm, welcoming image of diverse woman in natural setting (optional, can be replaced with organic shapes/gradient)

**Design Notes:**
- Use gradient background: primary-pale to secondary-pale
- Ensure text is readable on background
- CTAs should be prominent and above the fold
- Mobile: Stack elements vertically, reduce padding

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Clear value proposition in 3 seconds
- [ ] CTA visible without scrolling
- [ ] Mobile responsive
- [ ] Loads in < 2 seconds

### Section 2: Comprehensive Assessment

**Content:**
- **Headline (H2):** "Step 1: Complete Your Hormone Health Assessment"
- **What it covers:**
  - Current symptoms and severity
  - Menstrual cycle patterns (if applicable)
  - Lifestyle factors (sleep, stress, diet, exercise)
  - Medical history and current medications
  - Health goals and concerns
- **Why it matters:** Personalization is key to effective recommendations
- **How long:** 10-15 minutes
- **Privacy note:** "Your responses are encrypted and never shared"
- **Visual:** Screenshot or mockup of assessment interface

**Design Notes:**
- Use cards or expandable sections for detail
- Include visual of assessment UI
- Emphasize privacy and ease

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Clarifies what's involved
- [ ] Reduces anxiety about time commitment
- [ ] Addresses privacy concerns
