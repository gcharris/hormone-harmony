## B2C Hormone Harmony Site: Strategy and positioning

- Audience: Women 25–55 navigating hormone-related issues across life stages (cycle irregularities, PMS/PMDD, PCOS, fertility, perimenopause/menopause), seeking clear answers, trustworthy guidance, and privacy-respecting tools.[mckinsey+1](https://www.mckinsey.com/mhi/our-insights/blueprint-to-close-the-womens-health-gap-how-to-improve-lives-and-economies-for-all)
- Jobs-to-be-done:
  - Understand what symptoms mean and what to do next.[mckinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/mhi/our-insights/blueprint-to-close-the-womens-health-gap-how-to-improve-lives-and-economies-for-all)
  - Track patterns over time and see connections between hormones, mood, sleep, and energy.[careerfoundry+1](https://careerfoundry.com/en/blog/ux-design/a-beginners-guide-to-information-architecture/)
  - Make informed decisions—self-care, when to see a clinician, what to discuss with doctors.[reports.weforum+1](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Blueprint_to_Close_the_Women’s_Health_Gap_2025.pdf)
  - Feel seen and supported; avoid judgment and overwhelm.[womendeliver+1](https://womendeliver.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Women-Deliver-Brand-Guidelines-11.12.19.pdf)
- Positioning: Hormone Harmony is the approachable, evidence-informed guide for women to understand and balance hormones—with clear education, private symptom tracking, and gentle, actionable recommendations.[reports.weforum+1](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Blueprint_to_Close_the_Women’s_Health_Gap_2025.pdf)
- Naming/messaging hierarchy:
  - Product: Hormone Harmony Patch.
  - Byline: Powered by Rhithm technology.
  - Tagline: Find your hormone balance.
  - One-liner: Personalized guidance and support for every stage of your hormonal journey.[excitemedia+1](https://www.excitemedia.com.au/create-a-tone-of-voice-guide/)
- Differentiators:
  - Empathetic, non-clinical UX and tone designed specifically for women’s lived experiences.[womendeliver+1](https://womendeliver.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Women-Deliver-Brand-Guidelines-11.12.19.pdf)
  - Privacy-forward stance and plain-language data practices (no sale of data; consent-first analytics).[slickplan+1](https://slickplan.com/blog/information-architecture-trends)
  - Holistic education plus simple tools; platform is not just a tracker—it connects dots and suggests next best steps.[fullclarity+1](https://fullclarity.co.uk/insights/information-architecture-design-in-ux-complete-guide-2025/)
- Tone of voice:
  - Warm, supportive, clear; plain-language explanations; short sentences that reduce cognitive load; avoid jargon.[healthwatch+1](https://network.healthwatch.co.uk/sites/network.healthwatch.co.uk/files/2024-09/Tone of voice guidelines - updated September 2024.pdf)
  - Confident yet empathetic; optimistic and actionable.[hologic+1](https://www.hologic.com/sites/default/files/GWHI_BrandGuide.pdf)

## Information architecture and page outlines

- Navigation: Home, How It Works, Learn, About, Contact.[fullclarity+1](https://fullclarity.co.uk/insights/information-architecture-design-in-ux-complete-guide-2025/)
- Home
  - Hero: “Find Your Hormone Balance” + “Powered by Rhithm technology” + Start Your Journey CTA.[b13+1](https://b13.com/blog/designing-an-effective-information-architecture-best-practices)
  - Validation: “You’re not alone” + common symptoms (mood, sleep, fatigue).[mckinsey+1](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-insights/future-of-wellness-trends)
  - Three pillars: Learn, Track, Thrive with concise benefits.[slickplan+1](https://slickplan.com/blog/information-architecture-trends)
  - How it works: Assess → Understand → Act → Track.[careerfoundry+1](https://careerfoundry.com/en/blog/ux-design/a-beginners-guide-to-information-architecture/)
  - Trust: Privacy-first; evidence-informed; clinician advisors.[babylovegrowth+1](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/blog/site-architecture-best-practices-2025)
  - Testimonials: 2–3 brief quotes.
  - Final CTA: Take the assessment or join the waitlist.[b13](https://b13.com/blog/designing-an-effective-information-architecture-best-practices)
- How It Works
  - Assessment overview (what, why, time to complete).[careerfoundry](https://careerfoundry.com/en/blog/ux-design/a-beginners-guide-to-information-architecture/)
  - Personalized insights with sample cards/dashboards.[fullclarity](https://fullclarity.co.uk/insights/information-architecture-design-in-ux-complete-guide-2025/)
  - Recommendations categories: lifestyle, nutrition, sleep, stress, when to see a clinician.[fastercapital](https://fastercapital.com/content/Hormone-health-startup--Marketing-Strategies-for-Hormone-Health-Startups.html)
  - Ongoing tracking and progress.[fullclarity](https://fullclarity.co.uk/insights/information-architecture-design-in-ux-complete-guide-2025/)
  - Privacy and data control.[babylovegrowth+1](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/blog/site-architecture-best-practices-2025)
  - CTA: Start now.
- Learn (Resources hub)
  - Featured guides: cycle basics, perimenopause vs menopause, stress and hormones, sleep link.[mckinsey+1](https://www.mckinsey.com/mhi/our-insights/blueprint-to-close-the-womens-health-gap-how-to-improve-lives-and-economies-for-all)
  - Browse by topic: Cycle, Perimenopause, Menopause, PCOS, Thyroid, Adrenal.[b13+1](https://b13.com/blog/designing-an-effective-information-architecture-best-practices)
  - Latest articles and newsletter signup.[babylovegrowth](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/blog/site-architecture-best-practices-2025)
- About
  - Mission and story; women-first, evidence-informed.[womendeliver](https://womendeliver.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Women-Deliver-Brand-Guidelines-11.12.19.pdf)
  - Approach and values (privacy, empathy, inclusivity).[healthwatch+1](https://network.healthwatch.co.uk/sites/network.healthwatch.co.uk/files/2024-09/Tone of voice guidelines - updated September 2024.pdf)
  - Advisors/partners (as available).
  - CTA: Join the community.
- Contact
  - Simple form; response expectations.
  - FAQs: getting started, privacy, science, support.[excitemedia+1](https://www.excitemedia.com.au/create-a-tone-of-voice-guide/)
- Footer
  - Privacy, Terms, Accessibility; social; newsletter.[slickplan+1](https://slickplan.com/blog/information-architecture-trends)
