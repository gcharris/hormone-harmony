## Content Notes

### Tone & Voice
All copy follows these principles:
- **Warm, not clinical:** "You're in the right place" vs. "Access our platform"
- **Empathetic, not prescriptive:** "Understand what's happening" vs. "Fix your hormones"
- **Clear, not jargon-heavy:** "Plain language" vs. "Evidence-based modalities"
- **Confident, not arrogant:** "Science-backed" vs. "Revolutionary breakthrough"
- **Inclusive, not exclusive:** "Every woman" vs. "Women like you"

### Accessibility Considerations
- All interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable
- Focus states visible on all buttons/links
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum)
- All images have descriptive alt text
- Heading hierarchy is logical (H1 → H2 → H3, etc.)
- Form inputs have associated labels
- Link text is descriptive (not "click here")

### SEO Considerations
- H1: "Find Your Hormone Balance" (primary keyword)
- Meta title: "Hormone Harmony | Find Your Hormone Balance"
- Meta description: "Evidence-based guidance and support for women's hormone health. Understand your symptoms, track patterns, and get personalized recommendations for lasting balance."
- Primary keywords: hormone balance, women's hormone health, hormone imbalance symptoms
- Secondary keywords: perimenopause, menopause, PCOS, hormone tracking
