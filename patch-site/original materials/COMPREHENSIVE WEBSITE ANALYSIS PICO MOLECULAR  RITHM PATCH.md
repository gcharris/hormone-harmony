------

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**What It Is:** A sophisticated investment pitch website for Pico Molecular's flagship product "Rithm" - a continuous hormone monitoring wearable patch that combines microneedle arrays, electrochemical biosensors, and AI analytics to transform women's health care.

**Purpose:** This is a **vision showcase document**, not a product launch site. It's designed for private viewing by potential seed investors, strategic partners, and advisors. The site presents an aspirational roadmap of what the company could become with proper funding and execution.

**Status:** Pre-commercial, pre-clinical validation. Early-stage deeptech startup with strong scientific foundation seeking seed funding to advance toward FDA clearance.

------

## CORE VALUE PROPOSITION

## The Problem Being Solved

Women's hormone health is currently managed through:

- **Invasive blood tests** (painful, expensive, inconvenient)
- **Snapshot measurements** (miss the dynamic nature of hormones)
- **Reactive care** (treat symptoms after they appear)
- **Fragmented data** (no continuous monitoring or predictive insights)

## The Solution: Rithm Patch

**Tagline:** "Continuous hormone intelligence. Delivered with clinical precision."

**What It Does:**

- Painlessly samples interstitial fluid (ISF) through microneedles
- Continuously monitors hormone levels (estradiol, progesterone, LH, FSH, cortisol)
- Translates raw biomarker data into actionable insights via AI
- Provides real-time alerts, forecasts, and clinical workflows

**Key Innovation:** Moves hormone monitoring from episodic lab tests to continuous, wearable, AI-powered intelligence—like a CGM for hormones.
